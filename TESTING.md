## Limitations and corrections

- Job description wording about completed versus ongoing education is inconsistent; eligibility needs employer clarification.
- Work authorization and current course cost/access cannot be inferred from a skills match.
- Citations attached to learning exercises support broad skill areas, not the exact tasks or numeric targets.
- Generated technical explanations are practice material, not independently verified technical documentation.
- No course, lab, mock interview, or application is claimed as completed by the student.

## Reflection

The strongest improvement was converting broad advice into measurable work. The key review lesson is to distinguish source-backed requirements from generated exercises. A local Linux lab is an appropriate first learning milestone; actual completion should be documented separately.
